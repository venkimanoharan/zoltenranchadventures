❓ Pricing Questions

Common questions about our pricing

Are there any hidden fees?
No! All listed prices include the experience, guide, equipment, and basic refreshments. Any add-ons are clearly displayed upfront.
Do you offer discounts for large groups?
Yes! Groups of 6+ receive 10% discount, increasing to 20% for groups of 21+. Contact us for custom group rates.
What's included in the price?
Your price includes experienced guide, horse rental, all safety equipment, refreshments, and access to our facilities. See each package for specific inclusions.
How far in advance should I book?
You can book from 1-30 days in advance. For weekends and holidays, we recommend 2-3 weeks ahead for best availability.
What's your cancellation policy?
Full refund for cancellations 7+ days before. 50% refund for cancellations within 7 days. Reschedules are free anytime.
Do you offer gift certificates?
Yes! Gift certificates are available in any amount and never expire. Perfect for any occasion. Call or book online.
