❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our ranch experiences

📅 How do I make a booking? ▼

Booking is easy! Simply click the "Book Now" button on our website, fill in your information, select your preferred date and time, and choose your experience duration. You can book online anytime, and we'll send you a confirmation email within an hour.

⏱️ When do I need to book in advance? ▼

We accept bookings from 1 day to 30 days in advance. For peak times (weekends and holidays), we recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks ahead to ensure availability. You can also call us for same-day availability.

💰 What payment methods do you accept? ▼

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. Payment is collected at the time of booking online, and you'll receive a receipt immediately.

🔄 Can I cancel or reschedule my booking? ▼

Yes! Cancellations made 7 days before your booking receive a full refund. Cancellations within 7 days receive 50% refund. You can reschedule anytime at no charge by visiting your confirmation email and clicking the reschedule link.

🐴 What's the minimum age for trail riding? ▼

Children as young as 5 years old can participate in our family-friendly trail rides if they can sit confidently on a horse. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. We pick horses carefully to match each rider's size and experience level.

🎓 Do I need prior riding experience? ▼

No experience necessary! Our horses are gentle and well-trained. We have beginner-friendly trails and our experienced guides will teach you proper riding techniques. Whether you're a first-time rider or advanced, we have options for every level.

👕 What should I wear? ▼

Wear comfortable, sturdy clothing. We recommend:

  • Long pants (no shorts or skirts)
  • Closed-toe shoes or boots with a heel
  • Layered clothing - it's cooler than expected!
  • Sunscreen and a hat

We provide helmets for safety.

🌞 How long are the trail rides? ▼

We offer flexible options:

  • 1 Hour: Perfect for first-timers and younger children
  • 2 Hours: Great for families wanting a full experience
  • Half Day (4 hours): Includes lunch break
  • Full Day (8 hours): Our most immersive experience with meals
🛡️ Is horseback riding safe? ▼

Safety is our top priority! We use well-trained, gentle horses and provide safety helmets for all riders. Our experienced guides lead all rides and provide proper instruction. We have excellent insurance coverage and follow all safety regulations.

💪 What if I have physical limitations? ▼

Please contact us before booking! We work with riders of various physical abilities. Our staff can discuss accommodations, suggest suitable experiences, and ensure your safety and comfort. Many accommodations can be made with prior notice.

🤰 Can pregnant women ride? ▼

We recommend consulting your doctor before booking if you're pregnant. Generally, we avoid rides for women in their second and third trimester due to safety concerns. First trimester options may be available - please contact us to discuss.

💵 How much does a trail ride cost? ▼

Our pricing varies by duration, selected add-ons, and group size. The current package rates and automatic group discounts are always shown on our pricing page so the numbers stay aligned with checkout.

👥 Are there discounts for groups? ▼

Yes! Groups of 6 or more receive 10% discount on trail rides. Corporate team building, birthday parties, and family reunions get special rates. Contact us for a custom quote.

🎁 Do you sell gift certificates? ▼

Absolutely! Gift certificates are perfect for any occasion. They're available in any amount and never expire. Purchase online or call us. The recipient can book any experience on our website.
